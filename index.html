Getting started

Blue-AC180 reads nearby AC180 telemetry locally. Keep Bluetooth enabled and select your AC180 from the app device screen.

1

Enable Bluetooth

Turn on Bluetooth on your iPhone and keep the Bluetti AC180 nearby with Bluetooth enabled.

2

Select the device

Open the device menu and choose the AC180 unit. The list is filtered to devices whose name starts with AC180.

3

Open CarPlay

When connected to CarPlay, use Overview, Power and Battery to read the main values at a glance.

FAQ

Short answers to the most common setup and usage questions.

BLE

The app does not find my AC180

Check that Bluetooth is enabled on the iPhone, that the AC180 is powered, nearby and not blocked by another active connection.

W

Some values look different from the display

Blue-AC180 reads the machine telemetry registers and formats them for the app. Runtime is shown as decimal hours.

CP

CarPlay is locked

Blue-AC180 includes an initial CarPlay preview. Continued CarPlay use requires the Blue-AC180 Pro in-app purchase.

AC

Can the app control AC or DC output?

No. Blue-AC180 is a monitoring app. It reads telemetry and does not send control commands to the power station.

☁

Is a cloud account required?

No. The dashboard works through local Bluetooth communication with the AC180.
