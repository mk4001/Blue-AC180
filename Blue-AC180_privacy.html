Blue-AC180 Privacy Policy

Blue-AC180 is designed to read nearby Bluetti AC180 telemetry locally over Bluetooth and display it on iPhone and Apple CarPlay.

Last updated: July 4, 2026

Data We Collect

Blue-AC180 does not create a user account and does not collect personal information for MF-Lab servers.

Bluetooth Data

The app scans for nearby Bluetooth Low Energy devices whose advertised name starts with AC180. When you select a device, Blue-AC180 reads technical telemetry from the power station, such as battery state of charge, runtime, AC input, AC output, DC output, charging state, charging mode and temperature.

This telemetry is used to display the dashboard inside the app and on Apple CarPlay. It is processed locally on your device.

Local Storage

Blue-AC180 may store your selected AC180 device identifier, device name and purchase entitlement state locally on your iPhone so the app can reconnect and unlock purchased features. You can remove the app to delete this local data.

In-App Purchases

Purchases are handled by Apple through the App Store. Blue-AC180 receives purchase status information from Apple to unlock Pro features. MF-Lab does not receive your payment card details.

Network Access

Blue-AC180 does not require an MF-Lab cloud service to read your AC180. Network access may be used by Apple frameworks for App Store purchase validation, and by actions you initiate such as opening support email or external links.

CarPlay

When using Apple CarPlay, Blue-AC180 displays the same locally read telemetry in a CarPlay interface. The app does not send your telemetry to MF-Lab as part of CarPlay operation.

Children's Privacy

Blue-AC180 is not directed to children and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

This policy may be updated when the app changes. The latest version will be published on this page with a new update date.

Contact

For privacy or support questions, contact MF-Lab at support@mf-lab.com.
